Deep Geothermal Loop Simulator

A closed loop of steel pipe carries water 8 miles down into hot rock and back. The bare down-leg absorbs heat, the insulated up-leg keeps it, and a surface boiler raises steam for a turbine. Drag the sliders — everything recomputes live.

Physics: transient infinite line-source conduction in the rock, series radial resistances (rock · steel wall · insulation · Dittus–Boelter internal convection), exact exponential temperature march along each leg, Haaland friction vs thermosiphon buoyancy, and a pinch-limited saturated-steam Rankine cycle from an embedded steam table. Same model as geothermal/ground_loop_model.py. Loop fluid is constant-property pressurized water; the two legs are assumed not to interfere through the rock. A heat-transfer model — not a drilling plan. Note: the steam plant here is idealized — it ignores the boiler's internal pinch, overstating power ~3×; geothermal/co2_power_cycle_model.py models pinch-feasible steam and a transcritical CO₂ cycle that avoids the problem.
